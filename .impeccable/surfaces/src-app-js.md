# Surface: portfolio home (src/App.js), single full-screen scene

Mode: Experience (portfolio). Audience: PM/SWE recruiters. Action: email or open resume. Proof: nine dishes = nine real projects, plus two more in the list; experience written on the lid. Constraint: all copy verbatim. The visual system is in DESIGN.md.

Mapping (confirmed by user, 2026-11-15): curry chicken = AI Contract Review Platform, sweet and sour pork = Infoguard, fried chicken = TaskAllinOne, french bean = MaritimeRiskCare, cabbage = CloudPantry, eggplant = HeartSync, tomato egg = MakanSimi, steamed egg = ClearPass, egg tofu = AfterClass Hackathon UI. Artify and Cathay sit in the Projects list without a dish. Rice = About, curry and sambal pots = Skills, inside of the lid = Experience, red bag = Contact, QR stand = LinkedIn, voucher slip = Resume (Google Drive).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole portfolio is one open styrofoam cai fan box on a floating hawker table. Refuses the header + hero title + card grid portfolio, and refuses a scroll page with 3D as decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Flat sky blue, styrofoam white box, orange table, curry and sambal pots, a flimsy red plastic bag, steel. Flat toy-like 3D food, no faces. Rounded heavy lettering, plain grotesk copy, mono only on price labels and dates. Ink label pills pinned to the scene; white price labels with a colour square on each dish; foam panels laid out as box compartments.

STORY: Visitor sees one box with a name sticker on its lid, reads the labels, clicks one, flies to that part, and reads the content beside it. Picks dishes to see projects. Dabao closes the lid for Contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nothing but the scene. Box centred on the table with the lid standing open behind it, name sticker and experience list on the lid's inside, nine dishes over rice, sauce pots at the left, red bag, QR stand and resume voucher at the right. Seven label pills. A sound toggle at the bottom left. No header, no side title.

FORM: user-picked direction C of three previewed (hawker diorama, over the counter, styrofoam box); seed key d587eb9f from the earlier roll, superseded by the user's own choice.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the fly-in. Clicking a label eases the camera to that part and opens its compartment card; Escape, the back pill or empty sky flies out. Supporting: the bag trembling and leaning in a gust, generated sound effects, the lid shutting with a rubber band.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
